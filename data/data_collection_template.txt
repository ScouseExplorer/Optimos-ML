# TIPS FOR COLLECTING DATA:
# - Start with your top 50-100 most recent posts
# - Timestamps are most important (easy to get)
# - Engagement metrics are bonus (harder to get)
# - Even partial data (e.g., 200 posts with times) helps!

# WHERE TO FIND DATA ON LINKEDIN:
# 1. Go to your post
# 2. Click "Analytics" (below the post)
# 3. See: Impressions, Reactions, Comments, Reposts
# 4. Note the posting date/time (shown at top of post)

# POSTING TIME PATTERNS TO LOOK FOR:
# - Best hours: 7-9 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM (local time)
# - Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
# - Worst: Weekends, late nights
